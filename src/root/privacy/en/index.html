If there is any difference between this English version and the Japanese version, the Japanese version prevails.

2. Information we collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number and LINE / WhatsApp name
  • Details of your inquiry and the information needed for the procedure (nationality, status of residence, employer, family relationships, family register, assets, etc.)
  • Copies of documents you give us for the procedure

3. How we use it

  • To answer your inquiry
  • To prepare documents and file applications with government offices on your behalf
  • For quotes, invoices and communication
  • For identity checks and record keeping required by law

We will ask for your consent before using your information for any other purpose.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not share your information without your consent, except:

  • When we submit documents to the Immigration Services Agency, Legal Affairs Bureau, police, banks or other offices for the procedure you asked us to handle
  • When disclosure is required by law
  • When it is necessary to protect someone's life, body or property and your consent is difficult to obtain

5. Outside services

  • We use LINE, WhatsApp and Google Forms for inquiries. Information sent through them is also stored by those companies under their own privacy policies.
  • We may use translation services when communicating in other languages. We try not to enter information that identifies you, such as your name or address.
  • This site uses Google Maps to show our location.

6. Security

Gyoseishoshi lawyers are bound by law to keep client information confidential. We keep your information out of reach of unrelated people, store it for the period required by law after the work ends, and then dispose of it properly.

7. Your requests

You may ask us to disclose, correct, stop using or delete the information we hold about you. We will respond in accordance with the law after confirming your identity.

8. Contact

Please contact us through the contact form or LINE / WhatsApp on our site. We reply within 24 hours.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when laws change. Updates will be posted on this page.
