Message from the Founder

Haruka Kishi

Gyoseishoshi (行政書士) · Tokyo

At a school in Rwanda

Hello, I'm Haruka Kishi, the founder of HARUKA Gyoseishoshi Office. I'm a licensed Gyoseishoshi (administrative scrivener) in Japan, helping with immigration procedures and related legal documents.

I'm from Tokyo. After graduating from university, I studied abroad in Malta and traveled around Europe, then joined the Tokyo Metropolitan Police Department. Working as a police officer, I saw firsthand how many people struggle with worry and loneliness in their daily lives.

My friends from overseas have often told me how hard daily life and administrative procedures in Japan can be. Through them, I learned that the barriers created by differences in language, systems and culture are far bigger than most people imagine. Foreign residents in Japan, and those hoping to come here, still face many of them.

I hope for a world where everyone can live with peace of mind. I hope for a society where children can picture their future and hold on to their dreams, no matter where they were born or what circumstances they grew up in. I want to be someone who connects Japan with the world and with the future. That is why I opened this office.

Everyone's concerns are different. That is exactly why I take every concern seriously, no matter how small, and provide dedicated support to help you move forward with confidence.
