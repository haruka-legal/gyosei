If Your Application Is Denied

For applications we file, if the result is a denial we will review the reason and reapply once free of charge within 6 months of the decision. If approval is ultimately not granted, the remaining 50% is not charged.

This does not apply in the following cases:

  • Information provided was untrue, or unfavorable facts were not disclosed
  • Unpaid taxes or social insurance premiums
  • Required documents or additional materials requested by Immigration are not provided
  • Circumstances changed after filing (e.g. job change, divorce, legal violation)
  • The application is withdrawn for your own reasons before a decision
