<div class="guide-summary">

To leave Japan and come back with your status of residence, you need a re-entry procedure.<br>
If you will return within one year of departure, you can use the "Special Re-entry Permit" simply by stating your intention when you leave (within two years for special permanent residents).<br>
If you plan to be away for one year or more, get a "Re-entry Permit" from immigration before you leave (up to 5 years).<br>
If you do not return by the deadline, you lose your status of residence. This applies to permanent residents too.

<h2 id="without">What happens if you leave Japan without a re-entry procedure?</h2>

If a foreign resident leaves Japan without a re-entry procedure, in principle they lose their status of residence at that moment, even if their period of stay has not yet ended.

To come back and live in Japan again, you would have to go through the status of residence procedures from the beginning. Before a trip or a visit home, always check your re-entry procedure.

<h2 id="minashi">Returning within one year: the Special Re-entry Permit</h2>

Mid- to long-term residents who have a valid passport and a residence card can, in principle, use the **Special Re-entry Permit (みなし再入国許可)** if they return within one year of departure. Special permanent residents can use it if they have a valid passport and a special permanent resident certificate and return within two years of departure. You do not need to apply to immigration in advance, and there is no fee.

- **Deadline:** within one year of departure (two years for special permanent residents). If your period of stay ends before then, the deadline is the end of your period of stay
- **What to do when you leave:** On the ED card for re-entry (the embarkation/disembarkation record card), check the box stating that you are leaving temporarily and plan to return. Show it to the immigration officer at departure and tell them you want to leave under the Special Re-entry Permit

The deadline of the Special Re-entry Permit cannot be extended while you are abroad.

<h2 id="not-eligible">Who cannot use the Special Re-entry Permit</h2>

The following people cannot use the Special Re-entry Permit:

- People with a period of stay of 3 months or less
- People with Temporary Visitor status
- People designated by immigration, such as those whose status of residence is in the process of being revoked or who are subject to a detention order

Even if you cannot use the Special Re-entry Permit, you may be able to get a regular Re-entry Permit. However, the rules vary depending on your status of residence and individual circumstances, so check with immigration before you leave.

Please note that people with Temporary Visitor status, in principle, cannot get a regular Re-entry Permit either.

<h2 id="permit">Away for one year or more: the Re-entry Permit</h2>

If you plan to be away from Japan for one year or more, or have not yet decided when you will come back, get a **Re-entry Permit (再入国許可)** at a Regional Immigration Services Bureau before you leave. However, the validity of a Re-entry Permit cannot go beyond your current period of stay.

- **Validity:** up to 5 years (6 years for special permanent residents), within your period of stay
- **Types:** a single-entry permit and a multiple-entry permit that can be used any number of times during its validity
- **Fee:** at the counter, ¥4,000 for a single-entry permit and ¥7,000 for a multiple-entry permit, paid when the permit is granted
- **Processing time:** the Immigration Services Agency lists the standard processing time as the same day

Online applications are only possible together with another application, such as an extension of period of stay or a change of status of residence.

<h2 id="extension">If you cannot return by the deadline</h2>

If you cannot return by the deadline for an unavoidable reason, such as illness, you can apply to extend a Re-entry Permit at a Japanese embassy or consulate abroad.

- You must apply for the extension before your Re-entry Permit expires
- The extension can last up to a total of 6 years from the date the permit was granted (7 years for special permanent residents), and cannot go beyond your period of stay
- If a multiple-entry Re-entry Permit is extended abroad, you can use it to re-enter Japan only once. When you leave Japan again after returning, check again whether you can use the Special Re-entry Permit or need a regular Re-entry Permit

If you left Japan under the Special Re-entry Permit, it cannot be extended. If there is any chance you may not be able to return within one year of departure, it is safer to get a Re-entry Permit before you leave.

<h2 id="notes">Notes for people whose period of stay ends soon or who will be away for a long time</h2>

- **If your period of stay ends soon:** neither the Special Re-entry Permit nor the Re-entry Permit can be used beyond your period of stay. Before leaving, check when you need to renew so that your period of stay does not end while you are abroad
- **If you have applied for an extension or change of status:** different deadline rules apply while your application is under review. If you plan to leave Japan during that time, check with immigration before you go
- **If you are thinking about permanent residency:** being away from Japan for a long time may be viewed negatively when [applying for permanent residence](/visa/guides/permanent-residency-japan/) in Japan

<h2 id="help">Not sure what you need before leaving Japan?</h2>

The procedure you need for a trip or a visit home depends on how long you will be abroad, your period of stay and your status of residence. What matters most is checking, before you leave Japan, the date by which you must return.<br>
