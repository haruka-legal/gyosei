<h2>Personalized support directly from our founder</h2>

From your first consultation until the process is complete, our founder, a licensed gyoseishoshi, is your point of contact. You won't need to explain your situation again from the beginning. Instead of applying the same process to everyone, we plan each step around your circumstances and goals.

<h2>Support from a female gyoseishoshi</h2>

Our office is led by a female gyoseishoshi. You are welcome to discuss sensitive personal matters, including marriage and family issues, in confidence.

<h2>A gyoseishoshi who has lived and studied abroad</h2>

Our founder studied in Malta and later traveled across Europe. She knows firsthand how challenging it can be to deal with unfamiliar languages, rules and procedures. She draws on that experience to provide practical, considerate support.

<h2>Written communication in English, Spanish and Japanese</h2>

We offer consultations by email and chat (WhatsApp and LINE). We avoid difficult legal terms as much as possible and explain things clearly. Because everything is in writing, it is easy to review later, and misunderstandings are less likely.

<h2>Easy to reach across time zones</h2>

We accept inquiries 24 hours a day and generally reply within 24 hours.<br>
If you live in a country with a large time difference from Japan, please feel free to contact us. Even late at night in Japan, we reply right away whenever we can.

<h2>Consultations from anywhere in Japan or abroad</h2>

Our office is in Chiyoda, Tokyo, and we offer online consultations to clients all over Japan and abroad. We can assist you before you move to Japan, as well as with renewals and changes to your status of residence after you arrive.

<h2>No pressure to hire us</h2>

We first learn about your circumstances and advise you, including on whether you need a gyoseishoshi at all. You can also consult us without hiring us to handle your application.

<h2>Honest, realistic advice</h2>

If approval would be difficult in your current situation, or if you do not yet meet the requirements, we will honestly explain why and what you can do next.

<h2>Support for work, marriage, family and permanent residence</h2>

We assist with a wide range of immigration and administrative procedures related to employment, job changes, international marriage, family reunification, permanent residence, naturalization and starting a business in Japan. We can also help you plan ahead for your future in Japan, beyond your immediate application.

<h2>Clear fees, explained in advance</h2>

Our [fee guide](https://haruka-legal.com/visa/#fees) is available on our website. Before you hire us, we explain what is included and when additional costs may apply. An initial review of your situation is free of charge, and if detailed research or personalized advice is needed, we will explain the fees in advance.
